TechSaksham Internship
AI: Transformatie Learning – TechSaksham
(by Eduskills, Microsoft, and SAP)
As part of the
AI: Transformatie Learning
program under TechSaksham, I developed an AI-powered virtual health assistant to evaluate blood donation eligibility based on health parameters.
Project:
AI Virtual Health Assistant for Blood Donation Eligibility
Duration:
Jan 15, 2025 – Feb 15, 2025
Technologies:
Python, Streamlit, Scikit-learn, Random Forest, Pandas, NumPy, Imbalanced-learn
Machine Learning Model:
Built a Random Forest classifier using 1,000+ synthetic health records generated with custom rule-based labels.
Web Interface:
Developed an intuitive Streamlit app for real-time eligibility checks based on inputs like age, weight, BP, and conditions.
Oversampling:
Used RandomOverSampler to handle class imbalance and improve accuracy.
Chatbot Integration:
Added a rule-based NLP chatbot to answer common queries like low hemoglobin tips.
UX & Explainability:
Provided clear reasons for ineligibility and personalized health suggestions to enhance user trust.
