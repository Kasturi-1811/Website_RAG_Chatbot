AI Research Paper Summarizer
I developed an
AI Research Paper Summarizer
that generates concise and structured summaries of research papers directly from a PDF URL. The application extracts the complete research paper, processes the content using an optimized chunking strategy, and generates a final summary using a large language model.
The system uses
LangChain
with
Groq's Qwen3-32B
model to summarize individual sections and combine them into a comprehensive research paper summary. The chunking approach was optimized to preserve context across pages while reducing token usage and avoiding model rate-limit errors.
I built the application with a
Streamlit
frontend where users can provide a research paper PDF URL and receive the generated summary. The project also focuses on handling large research papers efficiently within the limitations of a free-tier LLM API.
Technologies Used:
Python, LangChain, Groq, Qwen3-32B, PyMuPDF, Streamlit
Domain:
Generative AI, Large Language Models, Natural Language Processing
Key Features:
PDF text extraction, context-aware chunking, multi-stage summarization, token-limit optimization
