SkillSwap – Mutual Skill Exchange Platform
I developed a website called
SkillSwap
that helps people connect and exchange skills with one another.
Users can post what skills they want to learn and what skills they can teach, making it a mutual learning platform.
This project was built during a one-day (9 AM – 5 PM) online hackathon. It promotes peer-to-peer learning by letting users search and connect with others who have complementary skills.
The backend was implemented using
Django
, and the platform allows account creation, skill listings, and user matching functionalities.
Technologies Used:
Django, Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript
Duration:
1-day Hackathon
